Bilingual upbringing

Living in a multilingual home is not always easy. Parents often lack guidance and support, and without a clear plan some children are left confused and unable to construct a coherent identity around their fragmented language skills. I’m here to guide both (future) parents and adult bilinguals reconnecting with their roots.
